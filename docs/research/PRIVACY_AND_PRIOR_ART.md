## Data minimisation proposal

Follow-up official verification: [European Commission GDPR principles](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/principles-gdpr_en) explicitly identifies minimisation, storage limitation and accountability. This supports the engineering policy; full legal-basis/health/cross-border assessment still requires counsel. [Sahana current Eden page](https://sahanafoundation.org/eden/) identifies Eden ASP as the main branch since May 2025; the earlier URL is legacy, not evidence the entire project is abandoned. [Ushahidi overview](https://www.ushahidi.com/support/overview/) describes separate client/server and map/list/timeline presentation; its version-specific stack is historical context, not a recommendation to adopt it. Lesson for Komşu: a list view is an operational fallback, and submitted reports must remain distinguishable from validated cases. Offline guarantees and incident data models require deeper source review before making comparative claims.

PII includes free text/audio, precise coordinates, contact details, device identifiers, health needs and rescue team movement. Do not copy CV or team biographical data into demo dataset. Synthetic locations are explicitly fixtures. Organisation controls sharing; initial product has no cross-tenant sharing. Training use is opt-in via separate reviewed, de-identified corpus. Content never enters application logs, metrics labels or error traces. Disk/backup encryption and access control are deployment obligations until tested provisioning exists.
